# Waterways Synthesis Science

Research to help better understand and manage artificial residential
waterways. An NCEAS working group.

## Projects

1. Systematic mapping of local and regional water quality effects
2. Spatial mapping and characterization
3. Water quality linkages of residential canals and HABs
4. Ecosystem indicators for monitoring and restoration
